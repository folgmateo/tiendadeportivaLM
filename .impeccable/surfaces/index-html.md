# Tienda (index.html)

Scope: storefront home + catalogue + cart + inline owner "Menú administrador" mode. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: general public in Guatemala, all ages, mostly on phones. Job: find a product, trust price and stock, send the order by WhatsApp. Owner job: adjust stock / prices / add products from the same page in admin mode (PIN, local to the device, export catalogo.js to publish).
Constraints: static, CDN libs, works from file://, no invented claims (delivery times, policies, reviews are unconfirmed).
Craft bar: Nike.com, On Running, Gymshark (user-chosen references). Owner follow-up: "more 3D, realistic, better photos, more flow, more streetwear but still running".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight: a premium sportswear storefront at Nike/On craft, where the product photography and the price carry the page. Refuses the toy-like illustrated hero, neon accents and decorative gimmicks of the previous version.

OWN-WORLD: White and #F5F5F5 product grounds, #111111 ink, Nike-grey #707072 secondary text, a single functional red #D30005 reserved for low stock. One family, Archivo: condensed heavy uppercase for display, regular widths for UI. Square product tiles on grey, pill buttons in black, hairline-free cards, monochrome wordmark. Streetwear running register: night-city campaign photography, near-black showroom band (#0b0b0b to #2c2c2e radial) for the 3D product, full-bleed editorial band; no brand logos of other companies in any image.

STORY: Visitor sees a night-city campaign image and one plain promise ("Corre la ciudad"), browses lifestyle category tiles, spins the 3D Street Runner shoe and picks its colour, filters the grid, adds to cart, sends the order by WhatsApp. Owner scrolls to the footer, opens "Menú administrador", enters PIN, and edits stock inline on the same cards.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Utility bar (WhatsApp ordering) + sticky header (wordmark left, five categories centre, search and cart right). Below, a full-bleed campaign photo filling ~85vh with the headline bottom-left in large condensed caps and two pill buttons (Comprar ahora primary black-on-white, WhatsApp secondary). Primary action: "Comprar ahora" to the grid; add-to-cart on every card.

FORM: canon (standing exit), seed: none (canon chosen) — round seed reference seed:972b8be4147ea98c. Signature interaction: the 3D showroom, a photoreal shoe (CC BY 4.0 glTF) the visitor drags 360° and recolours (Negro/Azul/Rosa) before adding it with that colour to the cart; supported by the pinned football spotlight that turns 360° on scroll. Motion grammar: quiet fades and image scale on reveal, sticky header that hides on scroll down, drawer slides; nothing bounces.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
